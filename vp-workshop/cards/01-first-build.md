# 1 · Your first build (13 min)

Goal: build a real, useful little tool -- and then change it just by talking.

## Build it

Paste this:

> Build me a single self-contained HTML file called `coffee-budget.html`: I enter how many people work at my company, how many cups of coffee each person drinks a day, and what one cup costs. Work out what we need and what it costs -- cups a day, kilos of beans a month, and the bill for a year -- as big numbers that update the moment I change anything. Make it clean, and a little bit funny. Then show it to me.

Wait for the preview, then try it. Put in your real headcount and watch the yearly
number land. Change one input and every number moves at once -- that's the bit
worth pointing at.

## Now change it -- without touching the code

Pick a few of these and just *ask*. One at a time:

> Make it dark mode.

> Turn the cups-per-day box into a slider I can drag.

> Add a bar chart breaking the yearly cost down by month.

> Put my company's name and a cheeky tagline at the top.

> Under the yearly total, tell me what else we could have bought with that money.

Notice: **you never edited the file yourself.** You described the change, Claude
made it. That's the right instinct -- describe the *what*, let Claude handle the
*how*.

## One thing worth seeing
Right-click `coffee-budget.html` in the file list -> **Reveal in Finder / Explorer**.

That's a real file on your computer. Email it, keep it, open it next year. It's
yours -- not a message that scrolls away in a chat.

## If something looks off
Don't fix it yourself. Just tell Claude what you see:

> The yearly total doesn't change when I edit the price of a cup. Fix it.
